# Source attribution and licence boundaries

The original Python implementation in `scripts/` is released under the accompanying MIT code licence. Reports and original figures in the public evidence package are shared under **CC BY-SA 4.0**, with attribution to participant **tomfng**. Source data, quoted text, source metadata and derived database rights retain their own terms; neither original-work licence overrides them.

- **Humane Intelligence / Zindi / Radiant Earth:** Bias Bounty Mapping Equity Challenge data, CC BY-SA 4.0 as documented by the platform. Bundled `data/metadata/source_README.md` records source attribution. Current challenge inputs have fixed URLs/hashes in `runs/discovery_scope/provenance.json` and the input manifests.
- **Overture Maps Foundation and per-record providers:** supplied place data and source attributes, including CDLA-Permissive-2.0 providers. Preserve original attribution and applicable per-source terms. Source README and per-record details remain available in the package and permitted inputs.
- **OpenStreetMap contributors:** road-derived evidence carries applicable ODbL obligations and attribution, as documented in the challenge source README. The report and figures do not waive these rights.
- **Census, CDC/ATSDR SVI, USFS:** tract geometry, allowed demographic/urbanity/vulnerability context and original challenge context, attributed through source README/manifests.
- **NCES CCD/EDGE:** selectively queried school identity/address/administrative-location/enrolment records. Public-domain statements are frozen in NCES item/layer metadata in the v0.4/v0.5 folders. Exact queries, hashes and retrieval dates are retained; external records are Discovery only.
- **Census Geocoder:** one previously selected supplied address, benchmark `Public_AR_Current`, retrieved 7 October 2026. [Census describes TIGER geospatial data as public domain](https://www.census.gov/newsroom/archives/2014-pr/cb14-208.html). Parameters, frozen response and hash are in the v0.6 folder. MAF/TIGER address-range interpolation is approximate; no road layer or reference inventory is reconstructed.
- **USFA/FEMA:** one exact Oklahoma FDID 42011 record from the official retired National Fire Department Registry, retrieved 7 October 2026. [FEMA's website-information policy](https://www.fema.gov/about/website-information) lists USFA as an official site and permits copying/distribution of copyright-free material, with credited restricted-content exceptions. No separate copyright notice was observed in this official CSV. This specific publication/reuse basis, the original download hash and selected-record hash are retained. No claim is made that every `.gov` publication is public domain. The source's reported pre-June-2026 currency and no-endorsement caveat are preserved.

The historical school safety-plan PDF, its full-page images and the contextual research draft are excluded. NERIS's separately governed contractor dataset is excluded. The core makes no historical evacuation-designation claim and no observed-incident/harm claim. Agency names are attribution, not endorsement.

No third-party credential, browser session, private message, full USFA national inventory, main-board trained model or external target reconstruction is included. The accepted numerical baseline is a separate permitted-data all-zero entry; external evidence does not enter it.

Original report licence: [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Source-specific terms, dates and attribution travel with redistributed evidence.
